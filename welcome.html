Anki Word Clipper

Read Estonian online and save unfamiliar words to Anki.

  1. Keep Anki Desktop open with AnkiConnect installed (Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons → 2055492159).
  2. Open the extension settings and approve the Anki connection.
  3. Optionally save an OpenRouter API key and enable translation. Without it, enter meanings yourself.
  4. Open an ERR article, or enable another site from the popup. Select a word or sentence, and click + Anki. Check the meaning, select a deck, and save.
  5. Sync Anki Desktop and your phone through AnkiWeb.

ERR is enabled by default. Other websites need your permission through Enable on this site. The extension sends selected text and limited sentence context to OpenRouter only when you request a translation. Article links are saved with the card, not included in translation requests.
